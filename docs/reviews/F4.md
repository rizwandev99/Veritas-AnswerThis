## Non-Functional Requirements (Section 12)

- **Performance**: In-memory graph construction completes in sub-millisecond time ($< 1$ms) per review using pre-fetched metadata.
- **Visual Polish**: Semantic color tokens (`var(--success)`, `var(--warning)`, `var(--danger)`) maintain WCAG AA contrast in both light and dark themes.
